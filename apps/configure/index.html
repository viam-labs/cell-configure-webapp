Place Approach

Geometry of the angled descent onto each pallet slot. height is the gripper's offset above the slot at the start of the place; angle tilts that start in the pallet's +X (or +Y) direction so a collision with neighbours deflects the box rather than jamming it. Pallet-config clamps the effective height to at least box_height + 10 mm so the held box clears same-layer neighbours during descent.
Inter-slot gaps. Pallet-config packs slots flush by default (box edges touching). A few mm of gap keeps the planner's collision check from flagging touching neighbours and gives the descending box room to settle.

Surface Dimensions

Geometry

Conveyor tilt — pitch incline tilts the long axis up/down (rotation around the short / width axis); roll incline tilts the short axis side-to-side (rotation around the long / length axis). Both write into frame.orientation.

Box Origin

Where the box sits on the conveyor, in the station's local frame (origin at the bottom-left corner of the surface, +X along width, +Y along length, Z = 0 at the top of the surface). Theta rotates the box around the vertical axis; the vacuum grabs the center of the box's top face.

Pick Home

Pre-grab waypoint: directly above the top of the box by this Z offset. The arm parks here, descends to grab, then lifts back here.
